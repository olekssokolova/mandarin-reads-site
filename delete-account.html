How to delete your account and data — MandarIN Reads

Effective 3 November 2026

Option 1. In the app (fastest)

  1. Open MandarIN Reads and sign in if you aren't signed in yet.
  2. Go to the Profile screen.
  3. Open Account.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm with your password.

Your account (email address, password, link to your subscription) is deleted immediately and cannot be restored.

Option 2. Without access to the app

If you can't sign in to the app (forgot your password, uninstalled it, changed phones, etc.), email us from the address you registered with and we will delete your account manually within a few business days:

mandarinreads.support@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • The email address and password used to sign in.
  • The link between your account and your subscription (RevenueCat / Google Play Billing).

What is NOT deleted automatically

  • Progress on your phone — saved words, your streak and the texts you've read are stored only on the device and are not linked to your account. To erase them, uninstall the app from your phone.
  • An active Google Play subscription — deleting your account in the app does not cancel it. Cancel it separately: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → MandarIN Reads → Cancel.
To stop being charged, be sure to cancel your subscription in Google Play as well as deleting your account — these are two separate steps.

For details on what data the app collects, see the Privacy Policy.
