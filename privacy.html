MandarIN Reads — Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

MandarIN Reads is an app for reading short graded texts in Chinese with translation and audio, made for people learning Chinese. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and how you can delete it.

What data is collected

DataWhyProcessed by
Email address and password (hashed) Signing in, so your subscription works on all your devices Firebase Authentication (Google)
Purchase ID and subscription status Checking for an active subscription, restoring purchases RevenueCat and Google Play Billing
Payment details (card number etc.) — The app never sees or stores them — payment is handled entirely by Google Play

Your reading progress, saved words, daily streak and display settings are stored only on your phone and are never sent anywhere. They do not move to a new device and are not linked to your account.

The app shows no ads and does not sell data to third parties.

How the app gets texts and audio

The reading texts are either built into the app or downloaded by a direct link from the host that stores the lesson files — an ordinary file download with no personal data sent. Audio is played either by your phone's text-to-speech engine or, where available, from an audio file on the same host — also without sending personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us (contact below) and we will delete it.

How to delete your data

You can delete your account right in the app: Profile → Account → Delete account (you'll confirm with your password). This permanently deletes your email address, password and the link to your subscription.

If you can't access the app, see the separate page How to delete your account and data or write to us directly.

Important: deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription — cancel it separately in the Google Play app (Payments & subscriptions), otherwise you will still be charged.

Data retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account, or no longer than needed for the subscription to work and to meet applicable legal requirements.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
